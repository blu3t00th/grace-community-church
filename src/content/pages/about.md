## Our Story

Grace Community Church began with a small prayer gathering and a desire to create a welcoming spiritual home in Stockholm. Over time, God has grown our church into a diverse family of people from different ages, backgrounds, and life stages.

## Our Mission

We exist to help people **know Jesus**, **grow in faith**, and **serve others with love**.

## Our Vision

We see a church that is spiritually vibrant, deeply connected in community, and actively engaged in the life of the city.

## Our Values

- Christ-centered worship
- Biblical teaching
- Prayerful dependence on the Holy Spirit
- Authentic relationships
- Generous service
- Compassion for our neighbors

## Our Beliefs

We believe in one God, revealed as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. We believe the Bible is God's trustworthy word, Jesus is our Savior and Lord, and the church is called to make disciples in every generation.
