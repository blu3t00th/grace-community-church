Prayer is not performance. Prayer is relationship.

This sermon focused on building a sustainable prayer life through:

- Simplicity over complexity.
- Consistency over intensity.
- Trust over striving.

Jesus invites us to ask, seek, and knock with confidence in the Father's goodness.
