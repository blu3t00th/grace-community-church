## Why We Give

We give as an act of worship and trust in God. Generosity helps us care for people, disciple families, and serve our city.

## How Giving Is Used

Your giving supports Sunday ministry, children and youth programs, pastoral care, local outreach, and mission partnerships.

## Ways to Give

- Online giving (provider link can be added)
- Bank transfer
- Special offerings for mission projects

Thank you for partnering with us in the mission of Grace Community Church.
