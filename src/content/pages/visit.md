## What to Expect

You can expect a warm welcome, heartfelt worship, Bible-based teaching, and a friendly atmosphere.

## Service Length

Our Sunday service typically lasts about **75 minutes**.

## Worship Style

Our worship is modern, congregational, and focused on Jesus.

## Kids Ministry

Safe and engaging kids ministry is available during the Sunday service.

## Parking

Street parking and nearby parking garages are available around Drottninggatan.

## What to Wear

Come as you are. Most people dress casually.

## Accessibility

Our venue is accessible, and our team is happy to assist with seating and practical needs.

## How to Get Connected

Visit the welcome desk after service or contact us online to take your next step.
