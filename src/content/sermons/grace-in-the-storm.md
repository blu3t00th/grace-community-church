When our lives feel unstable, God remains our refuge and strength.

In this message we explored three anchors from Psalm 46:

1. God's presence is our security.
2. God's voice is our peace.
3. God's kingdom is our confidence.

Take time this week to pray Psalm 46 daily and remember that the Lord is with you.
